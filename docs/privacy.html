Shade & Skin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026 · Applies to the iOS app com.vovanphuc.shadeandskin

Shade & Skin is a personal app built and operated by Van Phuc Vo. This page describes exactly what the app does with your data — no more, no less.

The three things that matter most:
  • Makeup try-on runs entirely on your device. No camera frame and no facial landmark ever leaves your phone.
  • The skincare features send data only after you agree, and you can decline and still use them.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with any third party for their own purposes. There is no advertising and no tracking.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, postal address or payment details. There is no account registration. The app does not read your existing photo library, your contacts or your location.

2. What “anonymous identity” means

So that your profile and plan survive reinstalling the app, it generates a random identifier and sends it with each request. That identifier is not tied to your real identity: we do not know who you are, and we have no way to find out.

3. AI processing

Your skincare plan and the reading of your skin photo are handled by an AI model we built and operate ourselves, running on our own infrastructure.

Your data is not sent to OpenAI, Google, Anthropic or any other AI provider. It is also never used to train a model.

Before any data leaves your device for the first time, the app shows a screen that explains specifically what will be sent and asks whether you agree. If you decline, your plan is produced by a deterministic rule engine instead, and no face image is transmitted from your device at all. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → AI analysis.

Consent is not just a checkbox in the interface. Every photo upload must carry an explicit AI-consent flag, and the server rejects any upload that does not carry it, before the image is processed.

4. What we never use face data for

Face data — camera frames, facial landmarks, the skin photo and progress photos — is used only to render makeup on screen and to produce the skin observations and skincare routine you asked for. We do not use it for:

  • advertising or marketing;
  • authentication, login or identity verification;
  • identifying or recognising you, or matching you against any other person;
  • building an identity profile about you;
  • training or improving any model.

5. Who else can touch your data

We do not share face data with any third party for their own purposes, and we never sell it. The skin photo is used only to produce the analysis you asked for; it is not sent to any external AI provider.

Our infrastructure hosting provider may technically process or store data solely on our behalf, as an infrastructure service provider. It is not permitted to use that data for any purpose of its own, and it is bound to protect it to a standard equal to what this page promises. Nobody else can reach your data.

There are no advertising networks, no third-party analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking.

6. How long we keep it

  • Makeup try-on camera frames: never stored.
  • Facial landmarks for makeup try-on: held in memory on your device for the moment they are drawn, then discarded. Never stored, never transmitted.
  • Skin analysis photo: used only for the analysis you asked for and deleted as soon as that analysis finishes. It is not kept as a permanent image.
  • Server-side analysis sessions: cleared automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity.
  • Progress photos you chose to save: kept only because you asked us to keep them. Automatically deleted 180 days after they are saved, or immediately when you delete them. Once deleted they are removed from our systems and not retained afterwards.
  • Profile, plan, check-ins, history: kept until you delete them.
  • Usage events: kept in aggregate, not tied to an individual.

7. Your choices

  • Withdraw consent: Settings → AI analysis, turn the switch off. From that moment nothing goes to the model.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Data → Delete routine and check-ins. This clears the data on your device and sends a delete command to the server, where it runs as a single transaction and removes encrypted photos as well.
  • Refuse the camera: deny camera permission. The photo-free skincare flow still works in full.
  • Ask us anything: write to the address at the bottom of this page.

8. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Progress photos you choose to save are encrypted on your device with a key held in the device Keychain, and the copy kept on our own server so it survives a reinstall is held under access controls we operate — it is not handed to any external service. The server authenticates the app with Apple App Attest, so only a genuine build of the app can download content.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

10. This is not a medical service

Every observation, score and suggestion in the app is ordinary cosmetic skincare guidance, for reference only. It is not a diagnosis and not medical treatment, and the app is not a regulated medical device. Talk to a doctor or dermatologist before making any decision about your health, and seek care promptly if you notice anything unusual on your skin.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced inside the app.
